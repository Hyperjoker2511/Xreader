I need sponsorship

Eillie
Fantasy Romance Shoujo
  • About
  • Releases | 25

Synopsis

When I opened my eyes, I was inside a novel and had become the director of an orphanage which abuse the main characters.

It was nice at first to be able to know when and where the abuse would take place and prevent it from happening in a cool way.

But now, the orphanage is on the verge of collapsing due to the mountain of debts accumulated by the orphanage. And all that is happening before the children are adopted.

I can’t starve the children! I need and will protect them!

After provoking the Duke, at the risk of her life, and even managing to get a sponsor. The only thing left for her to do was raise the children with love.

“Why are you only asking for them to be protected?”

“Contrarily to me, they’re weak and need to be taken care of.”

“Sielra, please don’t tell me that you’re about to end the sponsorship.”

He, who was only described as cold, kept coming to me.

This man whose cold expression keeps melting in front of me. Thankfully even the young children overcame their fears and opened their hearts to me.
