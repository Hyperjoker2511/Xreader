# Xreader privacy, in plain words

Xreader has no servers. There is no Xreader account, no analytics, and
no advertising. Nothing about your reading is sent to the developer.

## What stays on your device

- Your library, reading progress, bookmarks, history and downloads.
- App settings and customisation.
- Cached covers and chapter text (you can clear the cache in the app).
- If you sign in to a novel site inside the app, that site's session
  cookies — stored on your device only. The app never sees or stores
  your password; you type it into the site's own page.

## What leaves your device, and where it goes

- **The novel sites you use.** Opening a catalogue, book or chapter
  contacts that site, exactly like visiting it in a browser. The site
  sees your IP address and the pages you open, under its own privacy
  policy.
- **Repositories you add.** Adding a repository fetches its index from
  the address you gave.
- **AI features (only if you set them up).** AI features are optional
  and need your own API key (for example a Google Gemini key). When —
  and only when — you use an AI feature, the relevant text is sent to
  that provider under your key and their policy. With no key added,
  nothing is ever sent.

## Deleting your data

Uninstalling the app removes everything it stored. Clearing the cache
or removing a source inside the app removes that data immediately.
